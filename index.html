Stage 2: While-Reading

Read Alex's letter and explore the Present Perfect structure

Grammar Cues:
📍 London, UK

October 24, 2026

Dear Mom and Dad,

I hope you are doing well! I have moved into my new apartment in London, and everything is very exciting. I have lived here for two weeks now. My new roommate is friendly, and we have visited three interesting museums in our neighborhood.

However, life in a big city is busy. I haven't found a full-time job yet, but I have sent my resume to five companies. Also, I haven't bought a bicycle because transport is easy here.

Have you received the photos I sent yesterday? Has Dad fixed his car yet? I really miss you both!

With lots of love,

Alex

Stage 4: Live Oral Assessment

Ask these questions aloud in the virtual class to test student speaking and comprehension:

Prompt 1

"Has Alex found a job in London yet?"

Expected response: "No, Alex hasn't found a job yet."

Prompt 2

"How many museums has Alex visited?"

Expected response: "Alex has visited three museums."

Prompt 3

"What questions has Alex asked their parents?"

Expected response: "Have you received the photos? / Has Dad fixed his car?"

Prompt 4 (Personalization)

"Have you ever written a letter or email to your parents?"

Expected response: "Yes, I have written..." / "No, I haven't written..."
